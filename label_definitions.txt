# Hacker News comments, sorted by what each one offers in support of its claim.
# The hardest boundary is anecdote vs citation; the rule is in the README.

anecdote: argues from the writer's own experience, which no other reader can check
citation: argues from something external and checkable, such as a date, a figure, a named source, a quote, or a document anyone can go and read
assertion: states a claim confidently and offers no evidence of either kind
