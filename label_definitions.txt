# Hacker News comments, sorted by what each one rests on. The first test is
# whether a reader could check the claim without taking the writer's word for
# it; the second is whether it recounts something that happened.

anecdote: recounts a particular incident, whoever it happened to and whoever is telling it, that a reader cannot independently verify
checkable: rests on something a reader could go and verify, such as a named fact, figure, date, document, quote, event or rule, named specifically enough that a reader would know what to check
assertion: recounts no incident and rests on nothing a reader could check, such as an opinion, a preference, a habit, a prediction, or an argument from reasoning alone
