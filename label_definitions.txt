# Hacker News comments, sorted by what each one rests on. The test is whether
# a reader could check the claim without taking the writer's word for it.

anecdote: rests on the writer's own experience, where the only source is the writer
checkable: rests on something a reader could go and verify, such as a named fact, figure, date, document, quote, event or rule, named specifically enough that a reader would know what to check
assertion: rests on nothing a reader could check and no experience either, such as an opinion, a prediction, an analogy, or an argument from reasoning alone
